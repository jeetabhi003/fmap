# Before you pay a deposit: 20 checks for buying property in Mumbai

Research, not advice. Rules and rates change. Confirm each point on the official source named, or with a lawyer or chartered accountant, before you pay.

## The project

1. **Find the MahaRERA registration.** Search the project on the MahaRERA website. Match the project name, promoter and address to what the sales team told you.
2. **Read the proposed completion date.** Note the date on the MahaRERA page. Make sure the agreement for sale shows the same date.
3. **Read the quarterly progress reports.** MahaRERA shows the promoter's progress updates. Compare them with what you see on site.
4. **Check complaints.** Look for complaints and orders against the project and the promoter on MahaRERA.
5. **Check the developer's other projects.** Look up their earlier projects on MahaRERA and note whether those were completed on time.

## The home

6. **Ask for the carpet area in writing.** Under RERA, the price is for carpet area. Ask for the carpet area of your unit, and of any balcony or terrace, separately.
7. **For a ready home, ask for the occupancy certificate (OC).** Without an OC, you may not be able to move in legally. Ask for a copy.
8. **Get parking and amenities in writing.** Parking slot, club access and maintenance charges should be in the agreement, not only the brochure.
9. **Visit at different times of day.** Check traffic, noise, and whether the street waterlogs in the monsoon.

## The money

10. **Pay no more than 10% before a registered agreement.** RERA does not allow the promoter to take more than 10% of the cost as an advance before the agreement for sale is registered.
11. **Pay only into the account named in the agreement.** Get a receipt for every payment.
12. **Link payments to construction stages.** For an under-construction home, each payment should match a stage you can check.
13. **Check GST.** GST is generally charged on under-construction homes and not on ready homes with an OC. Confirm the current rate with a chartered accountant.
14. **Check stamp duty and registration charges.** Use the current rates on the IGR Maharashtra website. Budget for them on top of the price.
15. **Ask your bank about the project.** Banks keep lists of projects they lend to. If your bank will not lend, ask why.

## The paperwork

16. **Get a title search done by a lawyer.** It confirms who owns the land and whether anything is mortgaged.
17. **If the project is mortgaged, ask for the bank's release letter** for your unit before registration.
18. **Read the agreement before you sign.** Check the possession date, penalty for delay, and the specifications.
19. **Know the defect liability period.** Under RERA, the promoter must fix structural defects reported within five years of possession.
20. **Check your agent's MahaRERA registration.** Agents must be registered with MahaRERA. Ask for the number and look it up.

## Keep a file

Keep the brochure, every quote, every receipt, and every email or message from the sales team. Dates matter if something goes wrong.
