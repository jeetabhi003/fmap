# Sea-Face Legacy Buyer's Guide: small projects, sea views, and the questions to ask

**For:** Legacy / premium buyers (segment 3). **Length:** about 15 pages. **Price:** set after a test with 20 readers.

## Outline

1. **Why small projects are different.** Few units means fewer buyers at resale and fewer comparables.
2. **Confirming the developer.** How to tell a listed developer from a private one, and what to check when the record is missing.
3. **Registered sales as price evidence.** Reading registered deals and the difference between them and listings. The knowledge base shows three sources that disagree on Lodha Sea Face; the guide walks through why.
4. **Sea-face addresses.** What to verify on the ground and on the registry.
5. **Identity checks.** When a project name is used by two records (for example, Goshar The Legacy). How to resolve it before you trust a listing.
6. **Questions for the developer.** A written list.
7. **Next step.** Book a shortlist call (Layer 3).

## Source discipline

This segment has the most open items in the knowledge base, so the guide states "unknown" openly and does not fill gaps with estimates.
