# Worli Buyer's Checklist: 25 checks before you pay a deposit

**For:** End users (segment 1). **Length:** about 20 pages. **Price:** set after a test with 20 readers.

## Outline

1. **How to use this guide.** Verify on MahaRERA before you pay. Numbers here were checked on a stated date and go stale.
2. **Check the RERA registration.** Find the registration number, match the project name and address, and check the expected completion date.
3. **Check the status.** Ready, under construction, or conflicting. Show a worked example: a project where two sources disagree on status and why you wait for the portal.
4. **Check the occupancy certificate.** What an OC is, where to ask, and why "ready" without an OC is a risk.
5. **Check the price.** Asking price vs. registered price. Show how to read a range such as ₹6.42–18 Cr and why the gap matters.
6. **Check the locality.** Metro Line 3 stations in Worli (Worli, Acharya Atre Chowk). The 26 May 2025 station flooding as a case for asking about drainage and retaining walls.
7. **Check the developer.** Listed or unlisted, and what each means for accountability.
8. **Ten questions to ask the sales team.** Written, dated, and kept.
9. **Before you sign.** Agreement review, payment schedule, and what to keep on file.
10. **Next step.** Book a shortlist call (Layer 3).

## Source discipline

Each fact in the guide gets a source and a date in a footnote. Anything not confirmed is marked "unknown", as in the knowledge base.
