# Worli Investor Due Diligence: how to read a project before you buy to rent or resell

**For:** Investors (segment 2). **Length:** about 30 pages. **Price:** set after a test with 20 readers.

## Outline

1. **What this guide does not do.** It does not predict returns or name a "best" project. It shows how to check what is verifiable.
2. **Price per sq ft: asking vs. registered.** How to find registered primary sales, the gap between them, and why a gap is a question, not an answer.
3. **Developer check.** Listed companies, annual reports, and what a listing does and does not prove.
4. **Possession risk.** Reading the RERA possession date, and what happens when the date moves.
5. **Complaints on MahaRERA.** Where to find them and how to count them per project. The knowledge base marks this as blocked, so this chapter shows the method and the open status.
6. **Rental and resale evidence.** Using listings as a proxy, with the limits stated.
7. **Worked examples.** Three projects from the shortlist, each with what is confirmed and what is still open.
8. **Exit scenarios.** Hold, rent, resell. Write the assumptions down and test them.
9. **Checklist and template.** A one-page scoring sheet the reader fills in.
10. **Next step.** Book a shortlist call (Layer 3) with a licensed agent.

## Source discipline

Same rule as the buyer guide: each fact has a source and a date, and open items are shown as open.
