# Compliance and data rules

These rules apply to every page, email, ebook and lead record in this project. Check with a lawyer or a RERA-registered agent before you go live, since this document is not legal advice.

## Property sales (India, Maharashtra)

- Brokering a sale in Maharashtra requires a valid agent registration under RERA. Do not sell or take a commission until the business holds one, and put the registration number on every page that offers a property.
- Route Layer 3 handoffs to a licensed agent. The site does not take deposits or payments for property.
- Do not promise returns, appreciation, or rental yield.
- Show the RERA ID for every project named. Show the knowledge-base date for every price.

## Personal data (India: DPDP Act 2023)

- Collect only what the lead needs: name, email, segment, and consent. Ask for a phone number only at Layer 3.
- Record consent with a timestamp and the wording shown on the form.
- Never commit lead data to this repository. The repo is the product, not the database. Leads live in a Google Sheet owned by the business, with access limited to the people who work the leads.
- Give an unsubscribe link in every email and honour it within 7 days.
- Delete a lead's record on request.

## Accuracy

- Each claim is tagged with a source and a date. Unknown stays unknown.
- Before a fact goes live, check it on MahaRERA or the registry where the knowledge base says a portal check is needed.
- Do not name a person as a seller, buyer or endorser without their written permission.
- Do not present a project as "best", "guaranteed", or "sold out" unless a dated source says so.

## Sales content

- Do not use fake urgency ("only 2 units left") unless the count is verified and dated.
- Do not use fake reviews or testimonials. Use real ones with written permission only.
