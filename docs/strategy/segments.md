# Customer segments

Built from the shortlists in the Worli knowledge base (9 Oct 2026) and the customer-research method. The method says not to create a persona from fewer than 5 independent data points, so these are labelled as hypotheses until real replies arrive.

## 1. End user (buying to live in)

- **Job to be done:** find a ready home near work or family, without a surprise on possession.
- **Shortlist:** Lodha Adrina (ready, price verified). Sugee Marina Bay and Chandak Cornerstone are ready but still lack a verified price.
- **Top worries:** possession date, price per sq ft, and whether the occupancy certificate (OC) exists.
- **Evidence status:** hypothesis. Proxy source: the playbook's ready-possession criteria. Replace with replies from Layer 1.

## 2. Investor (buying to rent or resell)

- **Job to be done:** pick a project whose price and developer are both checkable, and know the exit risk.
- **Shortlist:** Godrej Trilogy, Godrej Trilogy Seafront, Prestige Nautilus, Birla Niyaara, Embassy Citadel, Runwal Raaya.
- **Top worries:** registered price vs. asking price, possession delay, and complaint counts (not yet collected for most projects).
- **Evidence status:** hypothesis. Complaint data is blocked until someone checks the MahaRERA portal.

## 3. Legacy / premium buyer (sea-face, low unit count)

- **Job to be done:** own a sea-facing home in a small project from a known developer.
- **Shortlist:** Lodha Sea Face (29 units, developer listed). Goshar The Legacy and D and A The Imperial Worli are held back until their developer records are confirmed.
- **Top worries:** resale liquidity in a small building, and whether the registered sale prices match the listing.
- **Evidence status:** hypothesis, with the most data gaps.

## What to do with the segments

Tag every lead with one segment when they sign up. Use the segment to pick the Layer 2 ebook and the Layer 3 shortlist. Re-check the segment each quarter, because the customer-research guidance says personas decay.
